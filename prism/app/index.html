Convert a visual

Drop in a graph, diagram, table or circuit — or start from a sample — and read, hear, navigate and extract it. Nothing leaves your browser.

1. Choose an input

Drag and drop an image here

PNG, JPG, GIF, WebP or SVG. You can also paste an image with Ctrl/⌘ + V anywhere on this page.

Or pick a sample

Fourteen semantic SVGs covering graphs, geometry, circuits, science diagrams, tables, flowcharts, maths and chemistry.

    What happens if…?

    Change a parameter and every representation updates.

    Refine

    PRISM cannot read text in raster images. Tell it what the labels say and the description, data and audio will use your values.

    Table cell contents can be typed directly in the Data tab.

    Recent conversions

    Your last ten conversions will be listed here. They are stored only in this browser.
